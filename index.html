From Chatbots to AI Workers

A 3-hour hands-on class built on Chapter 1 of Panaversity's The AI Agent Factory. In the first 90 minutes we learn to place any AI product on a four-rung ladder and to keep three ideas apart. In the next 90 minutes we give an AI a real accounts-payable job, and check every result against an answer key.

Humans define intent. AI Workers execute. Humans verify outcomes.

    What students can do

    Place any AI product on the ladder

    Search engines are queried. Chatbots are prompted. Agents are delegated tasks. AI Workers are managed.

    Keep three questions apart

    Does it remember and continue (continuity)? Which computer does the work run on (execution)? Where is the finished output kept (persistence)?

    Get 2026 value from the text box

    Write a brief with an outcome, context, format and constraints, so the box does the work instead of talking about it.

    Tell new from renamed

    Ask four questions of any announcement: which rung, where it runs and is kept, who it acts as, and its status.
