Opening · Why it matters

Maria's week

0:00 – 0:10

AI text boxes look the same as they did in 2023. What happens behind them has changed completely. In 2023 the box gave you back text. Today it can give you back finished work: real documents, spreadsheets and decks it can open again and edit.

Maria runs the office at a wholesale supplier in Ohio, about 40 staff. Click through her week.

Open the class with this story, before any definition. Let students guess what went wrong on each day before you reveal the lesson. Keep it to 10 minutes.

Mistake 1

2023 value from a 2026 system

Nothing on the screen told her the box had changed, so she asked for a summary instead of the finished result.

Mistake 2

Remembering is not keeping

The conversation remembered her, so she assumed the work survived too. She never asked where it ran, or where the result was kept.

Neither mistake was about intelligence. What would you have asked the AI on Monday instead?

Lesson 1.1

The ladder of interaction

0:10 – 0:25

Every era of computing has a unit of interaction: the thing you hand over and the thing you get back. When the unit changes, the skill you need changes with it.

Search engines are queried. Chatbots are prompted. Agents are delegated tasks. AI Workers are managed.

In everyday life. You look up pizza places near you (a query). You text a friend "Which one is good?" (a message). You ask your roommate to order and pick it up (a task). You put one person in charge of Friday dinners for the house every week (a role).

Climb the ladder

Agent

The technical mechanism

A model in a loop with tools. It plans, acts, checks the result and acts again.

AI Worker

A business definition

A role-based AI system. Click to see what it needs beyond an agent:

At work. One invoice register is a task. A register every week is a role. A delegated task becomes AI Worker work only inside a defined role, with responsibilities, authority and an accountable owner. An agent may be part of an AI Worker; not every agent is one. Several AI Workers under human management form an AI Workforce (Part V).

Game · Sort the jobs onto the ladder

Tap a job, then tap the rung it belongs on.

Each rung up moves more work away from you, and needs more control, because you hand over more authority and watch less. Land the operating rule here: Humans define intent. AI Workers execute. Humans verify outcomes. Play the sorting game as a whole class: students shout the rung, you tap.

Lesson 1.2

The same text box, a different destination

0:25 – 0:38
In everyday life. You text a friend, "Where should we eat tonight?" and get a name back. Then you text, "Book a table for four at 7 on Friday, under $120, and send me the confirmation." This time they go and do it. Same friend, two kinds of request.

In 2023 the box mostly led to a conversation. In 2026 the same box leads to one conversation that routes itself: a short question still gets a short answer, but a clear outcome usually gets work. Who decides? Often the system, mostly from what you write.

Lesson 1.3

Where the work runs

0:38 – 0:48
In everyday life. A contractor does your kitchen. Some rent a workshop for the week, some work in your garage, some have their own shop open all year. The cabinets get built either way. Where they are built changes what you can see and what is left behind.

When a request becomes work, it needs somewhere to run. An execution environment is that place: a file system, tools, a shell, and limits on what it can reach. It can be a temporary machine in the vendor's cloud, a virtual machine on your computer, or a computer that belongs to a long-running AI Worker.

The desktop-app bridge

The practical rule. Save anything you need later to a known place, and check that you can get it back. Know that place's retention and access rules. Maria's summary survived because it was part of the conversation. Her spreadsheet did not.
Durable is not authoritative. Personal memory belongs to one login; a shared project to a team. Neither is your company's official record. Chapter 4 adds three layers: KSoR (what a worker may know), DSoR (what it may do), SSoR (where the work stands).
Spend the most time here: this is what Lab Tasks 2, 5, 8, 9 and 10 test. Do the three-toggle widget live — ask "if I flip only this one, which answers change?" The answer is always: only its own.

Lesson 1.5

How today's tools realize it

1:05 – 1:15
In everyday life. Two banks both offer mobile check deposit. One puts it in the main app, the other has a separate app. Same idea, different packaging — so you check each bank's rules. Some people use a free, open app that works with any bank: more control, and more to check themselves.

As the book states them in early November 2026. Plans, betas and prices change: check each vendor's page on the day.

One conversation

Claude chat and Cowork are one conversation with no mode to pick; Claude decides answer vs task. Same Claude on web, desktop and mobile.

Where tasks run

Pro, Max, Ultra: in the cloud, in an isolated, temporary sandbox per session, destroyed when it ends. A scheduled task gets a fresh sandbox each run.

Your computer

Claude Desktop is the bridge to folders you connect, only while it is open. Claude in Chrome works in your own browser.

Where results are kept

Artifacts (Docs, Slides, Design…) in your account's Artifacts section. Plain files arrive as file cards in the chat. Undelivered working files are wiped with the sandbox.

Inside your files

Claude for Google Workspace (beta) edits Docs, Sheets, Slides in place; the result lives in the Google file. Add-ins for Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Outlook.

A worker that keeps going

Claude Duet: research preview, own virtual machine, morning brief, asks approval before outward actions.

One conversation, and dots

ChatGPT is coming together in one conversation; some apps still show a Chat / Work switch — choose Work for a task.

Tasks

An agent acts across apps and files and turns a goal into slides, spreadsheets, docs and websites. Built-in browser; local files via the desktop app.

Scheduled

Its own item in the left menu. In the desktop app, "Run on this computer" on = your machine must be on; off = cloud, which cannot reach your folders.

Inside your files

ChatGPT for Excel and Google Sheets edits live cells. Google Workspace apps create native Docs, Sheets and Slides in your Drive.

Dots

An always-on agent with its own cloud computer and browser, working between conversations, reachable in ChatGPT, Slack and Teams.

Rules & oversight

Custom Rules set what a dot may do alone, what needs approval, what is blocked. Activity View shows its work.

What changes

You install it and pick the model. No vendor sandbox, no help center: you own setup, updates and security. You pick the tool for the job.

OpenCode · for a task

Open-source coding agent. Runs as a background service on your machine; results are files in your own folders.

OpenClaw · for ongoing work

Self-hosted standing assistant; a proactive check wakes it every 30 minutes by default. Reached through WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, Discord.

Security

An early report found 30,000+ exposed copies. Run a standing assistant on a separate machine, with its own accounts and least permissions.

The invariant — on any runtime, four things stay true: (1) every request is routed to answering or to working; (2) the work runs in an execution environment, and you must know who controls it; (3) anything you want to keep must be saved to a known place; (4) durable state is still not the company's governed record.
Don't read the vendor tabs aloud — give 2 minutes per tab and ask students to find one fact that answers "where does it run?" and one for "where is it kept?". Then play the machine/record animation in all three modes.

Lesson 1.6

The paradigm and the products

1:15 – 1:23
In everyday life. Streaming replaced video rental. That shift was real even while one service's app was buggy and another had not reached your country.

The move from message → task → role is lasting. The products are less stable. Keep two questions apart: Is this a real shift in how work is done? (the paradigm) and Can I use it today, on my plan, in my market? (the dated product boxes).

Paradigm or product?

Four questions for any announcement

Pick one, let the class answer each question, then reveal.

Digital FTE. A worker doing a business process needs its own identity, its own limits and a named human owner. When it does one full-time role's work, the book calls it a Digital FTE.
The ownership rule. The platforms are rented. The knowledge and the controls are yours. Whatever lives only on a vendor's machine is rented too.
